01. Deposits and Payment Terms

Upfront Deposit

A 50% deposit is required before any build commences. This deposit is non-refundable once development work has started, as it covers time reserved for your project.

Final Balance

The remaining 50% is due on completion, once the build has been approved on your private preview link and before the site is deployed to your live domain.

Payment Methods

Payments are made via Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT). Work begins once the deposit reflects in our account. All quotes are valid for 30 days from the date issued.

02. Website Development & Monthly Maintenance Terms

Project Commencement

Web design and development will only begin once the 50% non-refundable deposit has been paid and all required client content/media is supplied.

Monthly Billing Structure

Web services are billed monthly via Zoho Invoice on a tiered structure based on utilization. Base Tier (R550/month): Charged every month if no client-requested modifications or updates are made. This covers server hosting management, active SSL security certificates, and keeping the site online and secure. Active Tier (R1,100/month): Charged during months where the client requests maintenance, content changes, layout modifications, or technical updates (up to an agreed hour limit).

Subscription Default

If a monthly invoice remains unpaid, we will send a reminder and allow 14 days to settle before the site is temporarily suspended. Your files and content are never deleted, and the site is restored as soon as the account is brought up to date. You may cancel the monthly plan at any time with 30 days’ notice, and we will hand over a full export of your site.

03. Ownership, Content & Revisions

Ownership of the Finished Build

On full payment, the completed website and its custom design and code belong to the client. Should you choose to move the site elsewhere, we will provide a full export of your files at no charge. You are never locked in.

Client-Supplied Content

The client is responsible for supplying text, images, logos and any other media, and warrants that they hold the rights to use them. IFIX-I.T SERVICES (PTY) LTD accepts no liability for third-party copyright or trademark claims arising from client-supplied material.

Revisions

Each build includes one round of revisions on the private preview link before going live. Further changes, or changes requested after launch, are handled under the monthly maintenance tiers set out in section 02.

Third-Party Services

Payment gateways, domain registrars, hosting providers and similar third-party services are governed by their own terms and pricing. Any fees they charge are separate from our build and maintenance fees.

04. Support & Service Standards

Post-Launch Support

We provide a 30-day warranty on the build itself. Any fault, bug or broken function attributable to our work is corrected at no cost within this period.

Uptime & Backups

Sites on an active monthly plan are monitored, backed up and kept updated. While we take every reasonable precaution, we cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability of third-party hosting infrastructure.

Scope of the Warranty

The warranty covers our own work. It does not cover changes made by the client or a third party after handover, nor failures caused by external services outside our control.

05. Corporate and Institutional Governance

B-BBEE & Tax Verification

A sworn affidavit verifying our Level 1 B-BBEE status and our SARS Tax Compliance status pin are available immediately upon request for supply chain vetting.
