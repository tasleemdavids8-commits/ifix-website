Our Story

About the Founder

I've always loved a good treat, and I know there are so many people who appreciate getting real value for their money. That's why I started Treats by Waheed — to create delicious Cadbury chocolate-covered Oreos that not only look beautiful but taste just as amazing.

Every order is freshly made with care because I believe my customers deserve quality, great flavour, and something I would proudly enjoy myself. Seeing happy customers return and hearing such positive feedback motivates me to keep growing and improving.

We currently specialise in Cadbury chocolate-covered Oreos perfect for weddings, birthdays, baby showers, thank-you gifts, corporate events, and many other special occasions — and this is only the beginning. There are many more delicious treats on the way.

— Waheed
